# **App Name**: Entrena con Diego

## Core Features:

- Home Section: Displays a compelling hero section with a clear call to action to schedule a free diagnostic session.
- About Me Section: Details Diego Jimenez's background in engineering and sports, highlighting his expertise in habit formation and athletic training.
- Services Section: Outlines the offered services, including healthy habits consulting and personalized training programs, with specific CTAs for viewing plans and rates.
- Unique Methodology Section: Explains the '4R Method' (Rigor, Resistance, Routine, Results), showcasing the differentiated approach to achieving sustainable health and fitness improvements.
- Contact Section: Provides a contact form for inquiries and scheduling strategic sessions, reinforcing Diego Jimenez's role as a trusted health advisor and personal trainer.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Forest green (#228B22) to inspire vitality and growth.
- Background color: Light green (#F0FFF0) with low saturation to provide a clean and calm feel.
- Accent color: Dark cyan (#008B8B) to add contrast and highlight key actions and information.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) for an elegant, fashionable feel; body text: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clarity and legibility.
- Use minimalist icons to represent services and methodologies.
- A clean, well-structured layout with clear sections for easy navigation and content consumption.
